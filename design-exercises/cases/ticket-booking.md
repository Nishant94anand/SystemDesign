# Exercise: Ticket Booking / Limited Inventory

Design a booking system for scarce inventory under high demand. Clarify correctness, reservation expiry, payment workflow, fairness, queueing, oversell tolerance and peak load. State invariants before choosing storage.

**First task:** explain how two customers trying to buy the last seat are handled.

**Later constraints:** payment succeeds but confirmation times out; reservations expire under overload; hot inventory partition.
